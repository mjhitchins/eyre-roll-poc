Methodology. Offence type, verdict, party names, and hundred are extracted by Latin keyword matching on F. W. Maitland, Pleas of the Crown for the County of Gloucester Before the Abbot of Reading and His Fellow Justices Itinerant in the Fifth Year of the Reign of King Henry the Third and the Year of Grace 1221 (Macmillan, 1884). Public domain. All structured data on this page derives from that printed transcription, not from manuscript images. Source code: github.com/mjhitchins/eyre-roll-poc.
